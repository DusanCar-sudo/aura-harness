Legal

Privacy, plainly.

This site has no accounts, no contact forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It also has no third-party fonts, scripts or images. Here is the short version and the slightly longer one.

Short version

Nothing you do here is profiled or sold. The only data involved is what your browser sends to fetch a page, what a mail client sends when you email us, and what you choose to tell us in that email.

Who runs this site

Lean Robotics IQ is the robotics-dataset line of Lean Progress IQ — a Praktess Research / Aura Code Project run by Dušan Milosavljević, based in Da Nang, Vietnam. Contact: aura@leanproiq.com. The research identity behind it is recorded at ORCID 0009-0006-4706-0467.

What the site collects

Nothing is collected by the site itself. There is no JavaScript analytics, no tracking pixel, no cookie, no localStorage profile, and no form that transmits data. The only JavaScript on the site runs the interface — navigation, tabs, and the two calculators — and it all executes in your browser.

Fonts are self-hosted from this repository (Archivo, Barlow and JetBrains Mono under the SIL Open Font License; Clash Display under the Fontshare free font licence), so no font provider sees your visit. Imagery is generated locally and served from the same origin.

What the server sees

Like any web server, the machine serving this site can see the IP address, timestamp, requested path and user agent of each request, and may keep an access log. During local review the site is served from a private machine; if the site is later published, the hosting provider's standard request logs apply. We do not build profiles from those logs, and we do not sell, rent or share them.

If you email us

We receive your email address, your message, and any attachment or dataset card you choose to send. We use it to answer you and to scope work. We do not add you to a newsletter, because there isn't one. Client data sent for a pilot is handled under the terms agreed for that pilot; we do not reuse it, train on it, or resell it. Ask us to delete a correspondence and we will, unless a legal or accounting obligation requires us to keep it.

Third parties

No third-party embeds. The only outbound links are to public research and identity pages — ORCID, Google Scholar, dataset project pages and the PDF of the preprint. Following one of those links puts you under that site's own privacy terms. The preprint PDF is served from this repository.

Your rights

If you have emailed us, you can ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. Write to aura@leanproiq.com and we will answer within a reasonable time. If you believe we have handled your data badly, tell us first — the address is a real mailbox, read by a real person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the previous version is kept in the repository's history. We would rather remove a feature than quietly change the terms under which your data was given.

This page is informational and not legal advice. It describes what the software actually does.
